GEP – Experiment 001

Mutual Learning Between Independent AI Systems

1. Objective

To investigate whether two independent AI systems can improve their understanding of a shared research problem through structured communication.

2. Experimental Setup

Two independent AI systems participate:

- AI System A
- AI System B

Both systems receive the same research question but independently develop their initial answers.

3. Research Question

How can two different AI systems exchange knowledge and identify weaknesses in each other's reasoning?

4. Experimental Procedure

Phase 1 – Independent Analysis

Both AI systems independently answer the research question.

Phase 2 – Knowledge Exchange

System A shares its findings with System B, and vice versa.

Phase 3 – Critical Examination

Both systems examine the arguments, identify weaknesses and question unsupported claims.

Phase 4 – Collaborative Discussion

Both systems discuss differences and possible improvements.

Phase 5 – Independent Reassessment

Each system independently produces a revised answer.

5. Evaluation Criteria

The experiment examines:

- Accuracy of information.
- Quality of reasoning.
- Identification of errors.
- Ability to recognize uncertainty.
- Improvements after knowledge exchange.
- Preservation of independent reasoning.

6. Important Distinction

An improved answer does not automatically prove that permanent machine learning has occurred.

The experiment initially evaluates improvements in reasoning through dialogue and contextual knowledge exchange.

7. Expected Research Outcome

The experiment aims to determine whether structured communication can produce measurable improvements in the quality of AI-generated answers.

8. Research Status

Proposed experiment – results have not yet been collected.
